SECOND RADIO LINK · multi-speed (Phase 2)
With two links, choose Auto under "Send via" in the chat. Tradewind measures delivery, delay and throughput on each link, uses the best one, falls back to the slower link when errors increase, and tries the faster one again after 10 minutes. PROBE in the chat tests both links. Speeds above 1200 need Direwolf (or another modem) and a radio data port that supports them, and 9600 needs a "9600" data port on the radio.
DOWNLOAD direwolf-tradewind.conf
Start Direwolf with: direwolf -c direwolf-tradewind.conf · Both stations must use the same speed and error correction.
Phones and other computers on your network can only open Tradewind with this PIN, so nobody else can key your radio.
Your position is the centre of your grid square, so a 6-character grid places you within about 5 km. Use a 4-character grid for a rougher position.
Modes are best guesses from each path: TEP = to South America across the equator (evenings); Tropo duct = 70cm, paths over 2,300 km, trans-Atlantic, or outside Es season; Sporadic-E = 2m, 800–2,300 km, May–Aug and Dec–Jan, daytime/evening.
Bulletins go out as BLNnWX (general), BLNnMAR (marine), BLNnAVN (aviation) and BLNnTC (tropical cyclone, sent with each new NHC advisory while a storm is within the radius or Barbados is under a watch/warning). Data: Open-Meteo forecasts and aviationweather.gov METAR/TAF/SIGMET. These are automated, unofficial summaries – for official forecasts and warnings use the Barbados Meteorological Services. Grantley Adams (TBPB) lies in the Piarco FIR (TTZP).
Commercial mode is only for a licensed commercial or emergency-services VHF channel (148–174 MHz), never for amateur frequencies, where encryption is not allowed. Use it only on frequencies your organisation is licensed for, and follow your licence's station-identification rules. In this mode APRS bulletins, beacons and APRS-IS are switched off; the chat is encrypted with AES-256-GCM. Message content is hidden, but the unit ID and the clear station ID can be seen.
To have Tradewind set the radio to this channel, tick Tune the radio under RADIO / TNC (CAT radios that allow it).
