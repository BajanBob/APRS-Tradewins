Robust FM sends the Robust waveform (8-FSK, ~44 bit/s, about 11 s per packet) through the FM radio in FM data mode. It decodes a few dB below 1200 baud on FM, but FM radios mute below about +10 dB, so for real weak-signal DX use Robust DX in USB. Never on 144.390 (shared APRS channel), 145.800-146.000 or 435-438 (satellites): Tradewind refuses those. Agree the frequency with the other station and listen first - it must be a free simplex frequency, not a repeater input. Robust and Robust FM stations decode each other; ordinary APRS radios cannot.
Needs the RTL-SDR tools (rtl_fm). Linux / Raspberry Pi: sudo apt install rtl-sdr. The SDR only listens - add a radio as the second link if you also want to transmit.

SECOND RADIO LINK · multi-speed (Phase 2)
With two links, choose Auto under "Send via" in the chat. Tradewind measures delivery, delay and throughput on each link, uses the best one, falls back to the slower link when errors increase, and tries the faster one again after 10 minutes. PROBE in the chat tests both links. Speeds above 1200 need Direwolf (or another modem) and a radio data port that supports them, and 9600 needs a "9600" data port on the radio.

TWO-WAY CHAT · APRS 2M / 70CM
Hail a station to open a two-way link. They get a call-out tone and an Accept button; when they accept, you confirm, and the chat opens.
#APRSTHURSDAY - the world-wide APRS net. Unlocked on Thursdays, 6:00 to 9:00 am
Your check-in goes to ANSRVR as "CQ HOTG" plus your text. The net's messages then arrive in the ANSRVR chat for 12 hours. It needs a path to the internet: your APRS-IS connection, or an iGate that hears you. At 9:00 am the feature locks and Tradewind leaves the net for you.

Calls are clear-text amateur signals and carry your callsign. Tradewind listens anywhere on HF but transmits only inside the amateur HF bands - your licence and band plan decide the rest. Selcall uses standard CCIR 493-4 for Barrett / Codan / Icom radios, or Gale - Tradewind's open calling mode - between Tradewind stations; ALE follows MIL-STD-188-141 2G and is meant to work with other amateur ALE stations (try it on air first).
